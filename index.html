Not a voice.

Hecate had been calling to me for months, but the moment I crossed that state line, I knew: it's time.

Because I don't just want you to "feel better." I want you to feel free. Free from the loops. Free to live the life that's been calling you. And free to weave ritual into your healing.

This isn't surface empowerment. This is shadow work with teeth and a torch in your hand.

Questions, answered
Who is this for?

Those who have done real healing and inner work and are standing at the threshold where the old self still runs the show. You don't need to be at the start of anything. You need to be ready to cross.

Do I need to know tarot or spellcraft already?

No. Each week's video and written guides walk you through the ritual, the spell, and the tarot spread, and the Hecatean Mirror Oracle Deck comes with the path. You learn it as you walk it. If you already read and cast, you'll go deeper with it.

Is this therapy?

No. This is initiatory shadow and spellwork, and it does not replace care from a licensed professional. If you are in crisis or in active treatment, keep your therapist in the loop and we'll work alongside it.

What if I miss a week?

Every teaching, ritual, spread, and meditation is yours with lifetime access, so a full week is never lost. Your private calls are scheduled with you and can be moved when life needs them to move.

What happens after week eight?

You keep everything: the teachings, the rituals and spreads, the meditations, the oracle deck, and your call recordings. The path stays with you and you can walk it again whenever you need it.
